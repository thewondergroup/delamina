Our story

A husband and wife, a residency at Shoreditch House, and ten years of cooking the food they grew up on.

Limor and Amir opened the first Delamina to cook the way Limor's family cooked at home, and look after people the way they'd been looked after. Three restaurants on, Limor still writes every menu, and her cookbook My Tel Aviv Table is on the shelf in each of them.

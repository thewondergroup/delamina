Our story

Delamina is Limor and Amir: a husband and wife cooking the food they grew up on.

Limor is self-taught. Her cooking comes from her father's kitchen and her family's Middle Eastern and European roots: plenty of vegetables, a charcoal grill, herbs and spices used generously. It started with a residency at Shoreditch House. Ten years and three restaurants later, she still writes every menu, and they still run it together as a family.

Her cookbook, My Tel Aviv Table, brings the same recipes home.
